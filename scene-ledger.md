# Scene Ledger

Running log of every lifestyle scene generated, so future batches stay fresh.

**Before designing any new batch:** read this file, then deliberately avoid repeating
the room type, design style, wall colour, and camera angle already used — for that
product, and ideally across products. **After each batch:** append the new rows.

Every batch must spread across these six axes (no two scenes share room + style +
colour): **room type · design style · wall colour · camera angle · time of day ·
accent-object family**.

Target density: MEDIUM — about three intentional styled objects per scene plus
breathing room. Not sterile-minimalist (reads boring), not eclectic-busy (reads
cluttered). Every scene gets a real wall colour — never an all-greige set.
