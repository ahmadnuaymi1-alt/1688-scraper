# Accent Bank

A growing, categorised bank of accent objects to style lifestyle scenes around a
lighting product. Used by the `lifestyle-scene-designer` skill.

**Rules**
- Never repeat an accent object within one product's 6 scenes.
- *Distinctive* accents (a record player, a cello, a vintage globe) carry weight —
  vary them batch to batch; don't lean on the same favourites. *Generic filler*
  (a stack of books, a ceramic bowl, a throw) may repeat freely.
- Keep **expanding** this bank — invent new objects every batch and add them here.
  The bank is the memory; a wide bank is what keeps scenes fresh over 100s of products.
- Match accents to the room, the product's style, and the palette — pull
  contextually, never as a fixed checklist.
